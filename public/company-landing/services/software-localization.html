Professional Software & App Localization

Expand your software's global reach with comprehensive localization services. We adapt your applications, websites, and digital products for international markets, ensuring cultural relevance, technical compatibility, and user engagement across all target regions.

Our expert linguists and localization engineers work together to translate user interfaces, adapt functionality, and optimize user experience for each locale. From mobile apps and SaaS platforms to video games and enterprise software, we ensure your product resonates with local users while maintaining brand consistency.

Localization Services We Offer

  • Mobile App Localization: iOS, Android, and cross-platform applications
  • Website Localization: CMS, e-commerce platforms, corporate websites
  • Software UI Translation: Desktop applications, SaaS platforms, enterprise software
  • Game Localization: Mobile games, console games, PC games
  • Help Documentation: User guides, FAQs, knowledge bases, tooltips

Our Localization Process

01
Internationalization Audit

Review code structure, identify hard-coded strings, and assess localization readiness.

02
Content Extraction & Translation

Extract localizable content and translate using industry-specific terminology databases.

03
Cultural Adaptation

Adapt images, icons, colors, date formats, and cultural references for target markets.

04
Testing & QA

Linguistic QA, functional testing, and UI verification on target platforms and devices.
