Project Overview

A rapidly growing online education platform needed to expand their global footprint while ensuring accessibility compliance and providing inclusive learning experiences for students worldwide, including those with hearing impairments.

The platform featured 450+ educational videos covering subjects from business and technology to creative arts and personal development. Each video required accurate transcription, timing synchronization, multilingual translation, and accessibility features including SDH with sound effect descriptions.

Our team implemented a scalable workflow that combined AI-assisted transcription with human review, employed subject matter experts for technical accuracy, and created culturally appropriate translations that maintained educational intent across all target languages.

The project resulted in a 235% increase in international student enrollment and helped the platform achieve full WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility compliance, opening doors to institutional partnerships with universities and corporate training programs.

The Challenge

Volume & Speed: Processing 450+ videos across 6 languages within an 8-month timeline.

Technical Content: Accurately subtitling complex terminology across diverse subjects from data science to creative writing.

Accessibility Standards: Meeting strict WCAG 2.1 AA compliance including SDH with sound descriptions.

Platform Integration: Delivering subtitles in multiple formats compatible with the platform's custom video player.

Our Solution

Hybrid Workflow: AI-powered transcription with human verification and editing for efficiency and accuracy.

Subject Experts: Engaged specialists in each subject area to ensure terminology precision and educational clarity.

Accessibility Focus: Implemented comprehensive SDH guidelines including speaker identification and sound effect notation.

Multi-Format Delivery: Provided WebVTT, SRT, and platform-specific formats with automated QA testing.
