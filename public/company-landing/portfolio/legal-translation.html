Project Overview

This comprehensive legal translation project involved translating complex international contracts for a Fortune 500 multinational corporation expanding into Southeast Asian markets.

The project required meticulous attention to legal terminology, cultural nuances, and regulatory compliance across multiple jurisdictions. Our team of certified legal translators worked closely with the client's legal department to ensure accuracy, consistency, and cultural appropriateness in every translated document.

The engagement spanned six months and covered over 500,000 words of legal content, including contracts, NDAs, terms of service, and partnership agreements across three target languages: Indonesian, Thai, and Vietnamese.

The Challenge

The client faced critical challenges with complex legal terminology, regulatory compliance across three countries, tight deadlines, consistency requirements across 500,000+ words, and maintaining the highest security standards for sensitive corporate information.

Our Solution

We assembled a dedicated team of certified legal translators, created a comprehensive multilingual terminology database, implemented a rigorous 3-tier review process, and established weekly collaboration meetings with the client's legal team.
