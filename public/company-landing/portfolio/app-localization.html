Project Overview

A rapidly growing fintech startup needed to localize their digital banking platform for expansion into Japanese and Korean markets, adapting both linguistic content and cultural UX patterns to meet local user expectations and regulatory requirements.

The app featured complex financial terminology, real-time transaction processing, investment portfolio management, and customer support integration. Every UI element required careful localization to maintain clarity, trust, and compliance with local banking regulations.

Our team provided end-to-end localization including string translation, UI layout adaptation for CJK characters, cultural UX consulting, date/currency formatting, linguistic QA, and ongoing support for app updates and new features.

The project resulted in successful market entry with 4.8-star ratings in both markets, achieving 2 million active users within 6 months of launch and establishing the platform as a trusted digital banking solution across Asia.

The Challenge

UI Layout: Adapting layouts for Japanese/Korean text expansion and character display requirements.

Cultural Finance: Localizing financial concepts and terminology to match local banking practices.

Regulatory Compliance: Ensuring all disclaimers and legal text met local financial regulations.

Real-Time Updates: Coordinating with agile development sprints for continuous feature releases.

Our Solution

Design Collaboration: Worked directly with UX team to create locale-specific layouts and components.

Financial Experts: Engaged local banking professionals for terminology validation and cultural review.

Legal Partnership: Collaborated with local legal advisors to ensure compliance and accuracy.

Agile Workflow: Integrated localization into CI/CD pipeline with automated string extraction.
